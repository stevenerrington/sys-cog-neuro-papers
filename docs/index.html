RankPaperRelevanceDays

    How papers are ranked. Each topic scores 3× its weight for a title match and 1× per distinct term in the abstract (up to 3). Method tags add a bonus (single units, LFP and fMRI count most), and the total is multiplied by species: macaque and marmoset ×2, other primates ×1.6, human ×1.25, rodent ×1.15. Open any paper's abstract to see its breakdown.

    Sources: bioRxiv (neuroscience, animal behaviour & cognition), Europe PMC (PubMed plus other preprint servers) and Crossref for the few listed journals PubMed doesn't index. Journal articles come from 89 listed journals in nine groups. Updated daily; journals, keywords and weights live in config.yaml.
